# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment
We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, welcoming, and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected Standards
- Demonstrate empathy, kindness, and professional courtesy toward other members.
- Provide constructive, respectful feedback on code and design reviews.
- Focus on what is best for the open-source hardware community.
